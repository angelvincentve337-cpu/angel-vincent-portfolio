About

Planning, drawings and approvals under one roof

I am a civil engineer who has worked independently since May 2018, handling residential, commercial and church projects from the first conversation with the client to the final submission set.

My work starts with site conditions, space needs and circulation, and moves through floor plans, elevations, sections, working drawings and 3D visualization. I also prepare the documents that building approval needs: permit and sanction drawings, K-SMART documentation, Layer Matrix and eDCR drawings.

I add quantity take-offs and cost estimates, and use STAAD Pro for structural checks when a project calls for it, while keeping my main focus on building planning and architectural design.
